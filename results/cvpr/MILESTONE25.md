## Requested wording that I did not use, and why

* **"Outperforms 11 published baselines."** Not true across benchmarks.
  * SigLIP2 is higher on BDD100K (0.622 vs 0.616) and on the Stratum 5 corpus atomic
    queries (0.337 vs 0.332).
  * SigLIP2 + metadata is higher on conjunctions (0.327 vs 0.310).
  * CLIP and ReferGPT-style are higher on the 4 val150 conjunctions.
  * Only five rows are re-implemented published *paradigms*; the others are
    foundation models, our own designs and lookups.
  * The paper says "leads all eleven alternatives **on human-labelled degradation**"
    and reports the ties and losses alongside.
* **Degradation effect numbers.** "−9.1 mAP / +7.3 ghost-FP" is the degraded-vs-clean
  partition of val150. It is presented that way in the abstract. SceneMiner's own
  top-25 is −5.7 mAP and −19.7 VRU recall, reported separately.
* **"Collapse to chance 0.217."** This holds for the lookup (and metadata) on object
  queries, and was measured, not assumed, for every other method.
  * BEV-TSR-style (0.208) and the tag map (0.198) are near that level on Stratum 5.
  * The image models are not (0.25–0.31).
  * The paper says so.
