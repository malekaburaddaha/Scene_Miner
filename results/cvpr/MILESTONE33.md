## Results (`scripts/incorporate_rater2.py` → `milestone33_rater2_results.json`)

### Agreement

"pos./neg. agr." is the positive / negative specific agreement. "Rater 2 confirms" is
the share of the 20 rater-1 positives drawn for that attribute that rater 2 also
marked positive.

| attribute | κ r1–r2 [95% CI] | raw agr. | pos. / neg. agr. | κ VLM–r1 | κ VLM–r2 | rater 2 confirms |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| droplets | 0.828 [0.73, 0.91] | 0.919 | 0.894 / 0.934 | 0.632 | 0.596 | 90% |
| glare | 0.760 [0.63, 0.87] | 0.919 | 0.812 / 0.948 | 0.456 | 0.387 | 85% |
| wet road | 0.883 [0.80, 0.95] | 0.944 | 0.929 / 0.953 | 0.670 | 0.613 | 75% |
| low contrast | 0.734 [0.62, 0.84] | 0.881 | 0.822 / 0.911 | 0.323 | 0.410 | 75% |
| **macro** | **0.801** (pooled 0.810) | 0.916 | 0.864 / 0.937 | 0.520 | 0.501 | |

**Humans agree with each other far more than with the VLM teacher:** κ 0.80 against
0.52 and 0.50.

**Correction to the task's premise: κ on this subset is not a conservative lower
bound.**

* Human agreement is *higher* on the VLM-boundary stratum (κ 0.811) than on the
  rater-1-positive stratum (0.762).
* Scenes that are hard for the VLM are not harder for people.
* I report κ as measured on an enriched subset and make no lower-bound claim.
